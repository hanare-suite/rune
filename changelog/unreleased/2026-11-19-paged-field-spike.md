- Spike, not for release: a `text_input` can flow its lines into pages (`El::page_flow`, `PageFlow`), take a text style and spacing per paragraph (`Block::Styled`, `El::paragraph_styles`, `ParagraphStyle`, with a forced page break), and mark runs with a font size and a colour (`Mark::Size`, `Mark::Color`). `Block` and `Mark` gain variants, so an exhaustive `match` on `Block` needs a new arm. `spikes/paged` proves it on a 51-page document.

- Spike, not for release: a `text_input` can flow its lines into pages (`El::page_flow`, `PageFlow`), take a text style and spacing per paragraph (`Block::Styled`, `El::paragraph_styles`, `ParagraphStyle`, with a forced page break), and mark runs with a font size (`Mark::Size`; a field keeps it as it keeps `Mark::Color`, once `TextEdit::from_parts` or `allow_marks` names one). `Block` and `Mark` gain variants, so an exhaustive `match` on `Block` needs a new arm. `spikes/paged` proves it on a 51-page document. `Block`, `ParagraphStyle` and `PageFlow` live in `rune-text` and `rune-edit` and `rune` re-export them; `Mark::Color` stays upstream's `Mark::Color(String)` (`#rrggbb`), which `Mark::Size` sits beside. `Block::same_kind` tells two `Block::Styled` apart by their style index, so `TextEdit::toggle_block(Block::Styled(2))` on a `Styled(1)` paragraph switches it to style 2 and toggles off only the same style. A field with `max_lines`, `truncate`, inline boxes or redaction ignores `El::page_flow` and `El::paragraph_styles`, and a debug build asserts.

- With `El::page_flow`, a paragraph that opens its page with the room above it from its paragraph style (the field's first, or one after a page break) stays on that page when it is taller than what is left, where it used to move on and leave the page blank; a paragraph that flowed onto a page drops that room and starts at the page's top, as before. A list marker starts where the layout starts the first line of a paragraph with its own indents, which it missed by a pixel where the two roundings differed or a hanging indent went left of 0, and where the paragraph's style has another font size than the field's. The caret of an empty paragraph, a press on it and the box a selected line break draws there follow its alignment and indents: centred and right-aligned ones no longer stand at the left edge, and an indented one no longer stands left of its own text start.

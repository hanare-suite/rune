- With `El::page_flow`, a paragraph that opens its page with the room above it from its paragraph style (the field's first, or one after a page break) stays on that page when it is taller than what is left, where it used to move on and leave the page blank; a paragraph that flowed onto a page drops that room and starts at the page's top, as before. A list marker starts where the layout starts the first line of a paragraph with its own indents, which it missed by a pixel where the two roundings differed or a hanging indent went left of 0, and where the paragraph's style has another font size than the field's. In a field with paragraph attributes or a page flow, the caret of an empty paragraph, a press on it and the box a selected line break draws there follow the paragraph's alignment and indents: centred and right-aligned ones no longer stand at the left edge (a centred one reading right to left is centred too), and an indented one no longer stands left of its own text start. A field without paragraph attributes or page flow keeps the caret of an empty line at its start, whatever its alignment. A rich field without paragraph attributes or a page flow lays its spans and blocks out again as before (a span an app keeps at its old numbers through an edit no longer leaves a paragraph with a stale layout), and in a field with them the empty paragraph of a right-to-left text follows its alignment (start, end, left, right) and the right padding of a code block with an indent of its own.

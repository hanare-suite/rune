- With `El::page_flow`, a paragraph that opens its page (the field's first, one after a page break, or one that flowed onto a page) with room above it from its paragraph style stays on that page when it is taller than what is left, where it used to move on and leave the page blank; and a list marker starts where the layout starts the first line of a paragraph with its own indents, which it missed by a pixel where the two roundings differed or a hanging indent went left of 0.

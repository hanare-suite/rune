- A `text_input` can be a word processor's editor. Paragraphs carry attributes beside their `Block` (`ParagraphAttrs`: a style index into `El::paragraph_styles`, `ParagraphAlign`, a left and a first-line indent, a page break before, an id the app finds its own data by, and a locked atom's height), read and written through `TextEdit::paragraph_attrs`, `paragraph`, `set_paragraph_attrs`, `set_paragraph`, `active_paragraph` and `format_paragraphs`; a split copies them to the new paragraph with the next id of `TextEdit::set_paragraph_ids`, and a merge keeps the first paragraph's. `El::page_flow` (`PageFlow`) lays the lines into pages, keeps a paragraph's first two and last two lines together (`PageFlow::keep_lines`, on by default), drops the room above a paragraph at the top of a page it flowed onto, and `Cx::pages(key)` reads the page count in the commit that changed it. Each page's top and bottom are `PageFlow::page_top` (and its end) in points rounded to device px once, so the text and the sheets an app draws from `page_top` agree to the pixel at fractional scales. A field with `max_lines`, `truncate`, inline boxes or redaction ignores `El::page_flow` and `El::paragraph_styles`, and a debug build asserts. A paragraph with `ParagraphAttrs::atom` (`TextEdit::insert_atom`) is a locked block showing its label: the caret steps over it, a selection covers it whole, deleting it is one edit, and accessibility gives it the group role. `Mark` gains `Size`, `Family`, `Highlight` and `Baseline` (`Baseline::Sub`, `Super`: 58 % size, a third of the em), and `Span` gains `family`, `bg_flat` and `baseline` with the builders `Span::family`, `highlight` and `baseline`. `TextEdit::set_history(false)` turns the field's own undo off and leaves Cmd+Z to the app, and every edit, changes of marks or attributes alone included, is reported as `EditChange`s through `TextEdit::take_changes` and `El::on_input_edits`. Rich text keys only the paragraphs an edit touched, as plain text does. Breaking: `Span` gains public fields, so a literal that names every field, or a pattern without `..`, needs them; `Mark` gains variants, and `ParagraphInfo` and `TextSystem` a field each. `El::atom_colors` colours an atom's box. Return in a paragraph of a field that carries paragraph attributes breaks the paragraph instead of submitting. `spikes/paged` checks all of it on 51-page and 200-page documents.

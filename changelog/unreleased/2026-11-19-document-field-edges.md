- A document `text_input` holds at an atom's edge. A rich paste over a selected last atom no longer panics, and one at an atom's end puts its marks and blocks on the pasted lines. Paragraph ids stay unique when text is typed over an empty paragraph before an atom or over an atom up to the next one, and `TextEdit::replace_text` numbers its paragraphs from the id counter instead of leaving them 0. Backspace at the start of the paragraph after an atom, and Delete at the end of the one before it, now select the atom whole, and a second press deletes it as one edit; an empty paragraph beside an atom still goes at the first press. With `El::page_flow`, a line taller than a page stands alone on its page and the next line starts the first page below it, where it used to be drawn over it. A field's atoms and its links no longer share accessibility ids: links take `0x8000..0xFF00` under the element's id and atoms the 256 above. The docs of `EditChange`, `TextEdit::take_changes` and `El::on_input_edits` now say that one event can report several changes, in order, each range in the text the change before it left.

- **A text field deletes, copies and pastes whole paragraphs as a word processor does.** A deletion that takes a paragraph whole, from its start to the start of the next, leaves the next paragraph's attributes, block and id on the line that remains; before, the deleted paragraph's stayed, so deleting the paragraph above a list item took the item out of its list. A copy that ends behind a line break ends in an empty `<p>`, and pasting it breaks the line there: a paragraph copied with its break is a paragraph of its own where it lands, and the line it lands before keeps its block, attributes and id. A locked atom copied whole is `<p data-atom="id" data-atom-height="points">`. `TextEdit::html_fragment(html)` gives what a paste would insert as a field of its own, and `TextEdit::paste_fragment(fragment, plain)` pastes it with its pills, and its atoms as atoms on lines of their own under new ids, so an app that keeps content behind pills and atoms can drop what it has none for, or rename a pill, before the paste. `paste_html` and `paste_html_with_pills` still paste an atom as its label's text.

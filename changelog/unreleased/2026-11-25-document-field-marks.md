- A document field's marks follow the allowed-marks set of the text editing model: `ParagraphAttrs`, `ParagraphAlign`, `ParagraphStyle`, `PageFlow` and `Baseline` are defined in `rune-text` and re-exported by `rune-edit` and `rune`, so the `rune::` and `rune_edit::` paths are unchanged. A field keeps `Mark::Color`, `Size`, `Family`, `Highlight` and `Baseline` once it is a document field, which `TextEdit::from_parts` with any of them makes it, and so does a paragraph-attribute method (`set_paragraph`, `set_paragraph_attrs`, `format_paragraphs`, `set_paragraph_ids`); a plain field keeps none of them, and `TextEdit::from_html` and `from_html_with_color` strip the ones they are not given. `MarkColor::new` snaps its colour to the 8-bit sRGB grid, alpha included, which is what its hex form in a saved history and in HTML holds, so `TextEdit::restore_history_json` takes back a history that holds a `Mark::Highlight` where it used to return `false`; snapping again changes nothing. The docs of `TextEdit::take_changes` and `El::on_input_edits` now say that a secure field's edits and `replace_text` are not reported. A formatting-only edit of a rich field (centring one paragraph) keys that paragraph, not every one after it.


- **A paste at the start of a line goes before it, and the line keeps its id, style, alignment, indents, block and lock.** `TextEdit::paste_fragment` (and so `paste_html`) used to give the line's id and indents to the first pasted line and a new id to the line's own text, to lose an empty line's style, and, behind a trailing line break or a pasted atom, to move a locked atom's lock onto pasted text or put the atom in an empty line's place. Now a fragment that ends in a line break puts every pasted line above the line, and one that ends open joins its last line to the line's text, which stays the same paragraph; a locked atom's line never joins pasted text. The pasted paragraphs are new, with new ids, the fragment's own style, alignment and block, and none of the line's indents (a paste at a line's end brings none either). A single line pasted into an empty plain line still gives it the pasted paragraph's attributes.
- **Return and a plain paste at the start of a line follow the same rule.** Return there adds an empty paragraph above the line, with a new id and the line's style, alignment and block, and the line keeps its id and attributes; before, the new empty paragraph kept the id and the line's own text got a new one. A paste of several lines there (`paste`, `insert`, typed text with line breaks) puts the pasted paragraphs above the line as new ones with the default attributes, and the line keeps its id, attributes and block, joined by the last pasted line when the text ends open. A locked atom's line stays whole and keeps its id. Undo and redo give the ids back.
- **The same holds when the text replaces a selection that starts at the line's start**, whether it is pasted, typed or a Return, so the line's id no longer depends on whether a pasted HTML carried a mark. **A fragment that ends in a line break and is pasted in the middle of a list item leaves the rest of the line in the list.**
- **A paragraph's id, attributes and block live in its line break.** Text that replaces whole paragraphs, from a line's start to the start of another line (typed, pasted, `insert` or Return, plain or rich, with or without marks), joins the paragraph whose break stays and leaves it its id, attributes and block; before, a plain edit left the removed paragraph's on it and a rich paste with marks left the next one's. Typing over `XYZ\n` in front of `end` gives `xend` under `end`'s id. A selection that ends inside a line still leaves the line it starts at its id. A paste or insert with a line break in the middle of a list item leaves the tail in the list, plain or rich.
- **`TextEdit::from_html` and `from_html_with_color` no longer make a document field of aligned HTML.** `<p style="text-align:center">` used to give the field paragraph attributes, so Return stopped submitting in a composer. Only a paste (`html_fragment`, `paste_html`) keeps paragraph attributes.
- **Text over a whole atom, or over a selection that ends at the start of a later line, gives one result on every path.** Typed text, `insert`, Return, `paste` and `paste_html` with or without marks (and `paste_fragment`) used to disagree: over an atom and its break the next paragraph's id moved onto the new text, and a selection from the middle of a line to the start of the next one made that next paragraph a copy of the first (its list bullet and style) under a new id. The paragraph whose break stays now keeps its id, attributes and block exactly, and in a field without paragraph attributes keeps its block too.
- **A bare line break pasted or inserted at a line's start no longer copies the line's style and indents**; only Return does. **A single line pasted into an empty line that has only an id** gives it the pasted paragraph's style and alignment again.
- **`rune::text::alias_family` no longer leaks its strings on every call.**
- **New paragraphs of pasted or inserted text are defaults on every path.** A paste or `insert` with several line breaks in the middle of a line used to copy the line's style and indents onto its middle paragraphs on plain paths, while a rich paste gave them defaults. Now the middle paragraphs, and a last one with nothing of the line after it, have the default attributes and the Paragraph block on both; what is left of the line after the paste goes on in its attributes. Return still copies them.
- **Pasting, inserting or typing over an atom's label without its break gives one result on every path.** The paragraph becomes text under a new id and keeps its style and indents, and pasted lines go above it as new paragraphs; before, a plain paste gave its first line the atom's style and a rich one the defaults.
- **Every path an edit can take gives one result, at an atom's edges and from one line to another.** Typing, `insert`, `paste`, `paste_html` with and without marks, and `paste_fragment` used to disagree in 161 cases of a probe over atoms and lines: a rich paste over the break before an atom left an empty paragraph, text at an atom's edge took the atom's style or a new id on one path and not the other, and the paragraph after a selection ended up with different ids. A rich paste is now the same edit of the text and the paragraphs as typed text, with the fragment's style, alignment and block on the paragraphs it brings. A join (a deletion or replacement that starts in the middle of a line, or covers exactly a line break, and inserts none) still keeps the first paragraph; whole paragraphs, up to a line's start, are decided by the paragraph whose break stays; and text with a line break over a selection from the middle of one line to a later one leaves the first line its own and ends in the later paragraph, with its id, attributes and block (in a plain field, `XA` stays a bullet and `Bnd` keeps `end`'s block). Text typed at an atom's start gets a plain paragraph, not the atom's style; only Return copies it.
- **A pasted atom is always an atom on a line of its own, whatever the selection.** A fragment's atom line that would have landed on an existing paragraph (the end of a selection that runs to a later line, whole paragraphs up to a field atom, the break behind a field atom, a field atom selected with its break) became text, or joined the paragraph's text, under that paragraph's id. A line break now goes on that side, the existing paragraph stays whole on its own line, and the atom has an id of its own. The break a fragment atom gets inherits no marks. Text over the break behind an atom leaves the next paragraph its own block, where it used to take the atom's.
- **An atom pushed down a line keeps its block, a pasted atom keeps its own, and pasting what is selected changes nothing.** Text or a break inserted before an atom (and a pasted Quote atom, which went in through a break before it) turned the atom into a Paragraph, or a bullet. A paste of a fragment that holds the selection (its text, marks, and the blocks and attributes of the lines it covers, ids aside) is now a no-op on every path, so copying a paragraph and pasting it back in place keeps its id and the data an app keyed by it. A fragment atom pasted over a whole paragraph before an atom takes that paragraph's place and leaves no empty line, and a fragment atom pasted into a code block stays an atom on a line of its own, the code line splitting around it, with the fragment's text going in as plain text.
- **An atom copies and pastes whole in a bullet, a number, a quote and a code line.** `selected_html` wrote `data-atom` only on a `<p>`, so a copy of an atom in a list item or quote read back as text, and a cut lost the table; it is now on the `<li>`, `<blockquote>` and `<pre>` too (an atom in code is a `<pre>` of its own), and `html_fragment` reads it from them. A `background-color: rgb(255, 255, 0)` with spaces keeps its highlight. Pasting what is selected leaves the caret behind it. `paste_html` of unformatted HTML with no plain text pastes unmarked text, as before. Whether a paste goes into code is decided by the line it lands in.
- **Return and Shift+Return over a selection.** Return over a selected line break changes nothing, as typing the break does not; elsewhere its new paragraph copies the style, alignment, block and indents of the line the selection starts on, not a mix of two lines'. A soft break is text, so Shift+Return over a selection deletes it by the rules for text (whole paragraphs leave the later paragraph its id and block) and puts a soft line, in its paragraph's block, where it was. In a field without paragraph attributes this differs from before in the blocks: text that replaces whole paragraphs leaves the later paragraph's block (it used to leave the first's), text with a line break from the middle of one line to a later one ends in the later paragraph's block, and the tail of a line split by text in the middle of a line keeps the line's block.
- **An atom is never a soft line and never has one.** Shift+Return at an atom's start or end (or over a selection that leaves the caret there) is a hard break, `selected_html` no longer folds an atom's line into a paragraph's `<br>` group, and a soft line read from HTML takes its paragraph's attributes without the atom and the id, so an atom beside a Shift+Return, or typed text after one, copies and pastes whole. Shift+Return over a selected atom deletes it with its break, as the deletion rule does for any text, and then puts the soft line where the selection was. In a field without paragraph attributes, deleting a whole soft line leaves the later paragraph's softness as well as its block (before, the merged line stayed soft).
- **An aligned atom in a quote or a code line copies with its alignment** (`text-align` on its `<blockquote>` or `<pre>`), so pasting its copy in place is a no-op. Cutting whole paragraphs that hold no atom and pasting them back at the caret restores them, ids aside. Marks a join into a code line drops (a code line holds none), or `set_blocks` to `Block::Code` strips, are reported as a change.
- **Pasting a copy of the selection is a no-op where the copied HTML carries the selection exactly.** The HTML cannot say an indent, a page break before a paragraph, a paragraph style on a list item, quote or code line, a soft line or a pill without the app's own hook, so a paste of such a copy follows the rules instead and gives the pasted paragraphs new ids. A copy that begins behind a line's end now starts with an empty paragraph, and one that ends behind a break, in code too, ends in one, so pasting it in place keeps the lines apart.
- **An atom is never a soft line and never has one, after any edit.** `sync_invariants` drops a soft line that is an atom or that follows one, so pastes, deletions and Return beside an atom and a soft line keep the rule that Shift+Return alone kept. A fragment that ends in an atom, pasted at an empty line, leaves that line whole, with its id, attributes and page break, as before (only a single line pasted into an empty plain Paragraph line takes the line).
- **A fragment with no text but formatting no longer strips an atom or an empty line.** `paste_html` of an aligned empty paragraph, an empty list item or quote, or an image the parser drops, pasted at an atom's start, made the atom text under a new id, unreported. A fragment with no text pasted at a caret now changes nothing (only a single line pasted into an empty plain line gives it its attributes, as before), and over a selection it is the plain deletion.
- **A line a paste creates or joins in a code block is code.** Position decides, and code wins: on an empty code line, at a code line's start or end, or inside a run, pasted HTML lands as plain text in code lines, with the whitespace of the plain fallback, no marks and no style, as before; a line of the same paste that ends up in a paragraph keeps the fragment's formatting (bold `X` stays bold in `abX` while `Y` goes into the code line plain). Cutting a paragraph that stands next to a code block and pasting it back is not promised: the line is made beside code, so it is code.
- **Where a copy begins and ends.** A copy that begins at a line's end starts with a plain `<p></p>`, never an empty `<li>`, `<blockquote>` or `<pre>`, so other apps show no empty bullet or quote above the paste, and one that begins at a code line's end keeps its leading break. A formatted copy that ends behind a break, pasted into a single-line rich field, no longer adds a trailing space ("xyone twozw"). **A soft line that `sync_invariants` drops** (an atom beside it) is reported as a paragraph change, wherever it is.
- **Pasting into code takes the plain fallback as the field takes plain text.** `paste_fragment` and `paste_html` read a CRLF fallback as line feeds, so no carriage return reaches a code line; a fallback with other lines than the fragment (Chrome's blank lines and trailing break), pasted where the first line is code and with no atom, is pasted whole, as before; and atoms among code lines take the fallback's text for the code lines by matching its non-empty lines, less the atoms' labels, to the fragment's text lines, or keep the fragment's collapsed text where they do not match. A fragment's soft break is no soft line of code.
- **Unformatted text pasted at a paragraph's start: the lines that become paragraphs of their own take no marks from the paragraph that starts there, and text at a link's start is not in the link.** Cutting `body` before a bold or linked `Term rest` and pasting it back made `body` bold or linked. In a document an unformatted paste with a line break at a line's start now gives the lines it makes new paragraphs of no marks; an open last line, which joins the receiving paragraph, inherits as typed text does (so its run size is kept), and the caret before a paragraph's first character no longer inherits a link (it still inherits bold and the like, as in a word processor). The paste is one undo step.
- **The end of the text counts as the last paragraph's break.** Selecting from a line's start to the end of the document and cutting it leaves the last paragraph's empty line, with its id and attributes, by the whole-paragraph rule, and pasting back restores that id on that paragraph. When the last paragraph has soft lines, the paragraph that stays is the hard line they belong to, not the soft line itself. In plain fields, Select All then typing or Backspace now leaves the last line's block (Code, say), where dev leaves the first line's. When the last paragraph is an atom, no existing id survives.
- **A line's softness describes the break before it.** When lines merge, the merged line is as soft as the first of them, never as the later one: deleting to the end of the document no longer glues the leftover line to the paragraph above as a soft line (`<p>ab<br></p>`), and deleting `cd\n` in front of a soft `ef` leaves `ef` hard, as on dev. A soft line the edit starts on keeps the block of the paragraph above.
- **Which lines of a paste are code is read from the paste itself.** `paste_fragment` does the paste to a copy of the field (when it holds code) and reads the lines it made, so an atom whose block is Code, an atom beside code lines, and a one-line paste from an atom's end into a code line no longer get a Code line with collapsed text, and the plain fallback is pasted whole only where the paste's first line is code. `rune::text::alias_family` leaks each distinct string once, even when a name alternates between two families.
- **A paragraph with a soft line cut and pasted back is restored, soft line included.** A fragment with soft breaks is no unformatted text, so it takes the rich route and keeps them (it pasted as plain text, with hard lines, before). A copy that ends at the start of a soft line says so (`a<br><br>`, as an empty soft line at the end of a block needs a second `<br>`), so pasting it back leaves that line soft.
- **A soft line is part of its paragraph, and the paragraph is its head (the hard line its soft lines follow).** One rule replaces the case-by-case handling of soft lines. A soft line always has its head's block, style, alignment and indents: `sync_invariants` (and the end of every edit) enforces it as it does the atom rule, and reports a line it changed as a paragraph change. A soft line under a code head is code, and one under any other head never is. A soft line's start is the middle of its paragraph: the line-start, whole-paragraph, mid-to-mid and front rules look at hard lines' starts only, so text with a line break inserted at a soft line's start goes into the paragraph, the head keeping its id, attributes and block, and deleting `cd\n` before a hard `ef` when `cd` is a soft line starts mid-paragraph. A paragraph's id is its head's. A fragment's soft line that lands on a kept line makes the receiving paragraph the head: pasting `x<br>y` at the start of a centred bullet `ef` gives `x` (the bullet's id, centred, bullet) and a mirrored soft line `yef` with an id of its own, as typing `x`, `Shift+Return`, `y` gives. Differences from dev, in plain fields too: the soft lines of a Quote that Backspace joins into a Paragraph become Paragraph lines (dev left them Quote lines); a soft line first in a list item that `Return` finds empty is a break, not the end of the list; text typed or pasted over a selected soft break is a hard break, and a paste of the selection's own text over soft lines is a change when it differs in softness.
- **A paste or a Shift+Return that changes nothing reports nothing.** Pasting a selection's own copy over a centred code line, or `Shift+Return` over a selected soft break, no longer reports a change. A copy of a selection that ends at the start of a soft line, or begins at the end of the line before one, says so in its HTML.
- **Formatting commands act on whole paragraphs.** `set_block`, `toggle_block`, `indent_list`, `outdent_list`, `format_paragraphs` (and `active_block`, `selected_lines`) widen the selection up to the head and down through the trailing soft lines, so a caret on a soft line formats its paragraph and the soft lines follow the head. `set_paragraph` on a soft line sets the head's paragraph, under the head's id. `indent_list` returns `true` only when it indented something (it was `true` for any list item, also at the deepest level).
- **A fragment of soft lines pastes as the same text typed with `Shift+Return`.** Inside a paragraph the head keeps its id (`ab`, `x<br>y` at the caret after `a` gives `ax` under `ab`'s id); only where the paste starts a paragraph does the receiving paragraph become the head of the group. A block set on a head that makes its soft lines code takes their marks off in the same undo step.
- **`indent_list` and `outdent_list` say whether they changed anything.** Both return `true` only when a list item moved a level; at the deepest level and at level 0 they return `false` and report nothing. `Tab` and `Shift+Tab` in a list item stay the document's at those levels (the runtime keeps them from moving focus whenever the caret is in a list item).
- **A pasted soft line never goes on an atom's line, or the line after one.** A fragment that ends in a soft line (a copy of `one` through its soft break) pasted at a table atom's start used to put `one` on the table's line, under the table's id and lock, and give the table a new id. The soft break is dropped there and the receiving paragraph does not head the group. A line that was there and becomes a pasted soft line has a new id and no page break, as typed text gives.
- **Pasting the selection's own copy is a no-op in two more places.** Where a mark spans a line break (bold across two paragraphs, copied and pasted in place), the break's byte no longer counts, so the ids and the centring stay. Next to code the no-op rule comes first: a paragraph with a soft line before a code line, or a bullet with one between code lines, is not turned into code.
- **A copy that starts at an empty code line keeps that line.** The HTML parser drops the newline after `<pre><code>`, so the copy writes another when the block's first line is empty. Pasting a selection's own copy over a selection that touches code is a no-op only where the plain text is the selection too (or empty): a foreign paste over code goes in as its plain text. An own copy that ends at the start of its last line, a later one than its first, does not count that line as selected.
- **A line with no text is no atom.** `sync_invariants` clears the atom of an empty line (it gets an id of its own), including after `set_paragraph_attrs` and `set_paragraph`. `paste_html` makes an atom of the HTML its label as text: it is the path for a foreign clipboard, and an app pastes its own atoms through `paste_fragment`.
- **Nothing replaced by nothing is nothing, with a style armed too.** `insert("")`, `paste("")`, an empty `paste_html` and `paste_html` of markup with no text no longer turn an atom into text when the caret is at its start and a style (bold, say) is armed: they report nothing, record no undo step and keep the armed style. `insert_atom` at an atom's start puts the new atom on a line of its own before it and leaves the existing one alone.
- **What remains of a paragraph behind inserted text keeps the paragraph's attributes and block.** At the end of a paragraph's first line, the soft lines that follow count as the rest of the paragraph, whether or not the inserted text ends in a break (`x\ny`, Return, `"\n"`): the line behind the break and the soft lines keep the paragraph's block (and, in a document, its attributes, the new line under a fresh id), in plain fields too, where soft lines behind such a break used to fall back to the Paragraph block. This is the mid-line case's rule (`yb` keeps them).
- **Smaller:** a mark command over code (`toggle_mark(Bold)` on a code line) reports no change when the marks did not change. `set_paragraph_ids` gives an id to a line that has none when ids are turned on (an atom cleared for want of a label before the ids), so the order of `set_paragraph_attrs` and `set_paragraph_ids` does not matter. An empty fragment over a break beside an atom arms no typing style and reports nothing. `to_html` of a document that ends in an empty code line round-trips through `from_html` (the last block says its last empty line with another newline); the trailing `<p></p>` is only for copies that end behind a break into a non-code line.
- **`insert_atom` always makes a new paragraph.** The atom has a fresh id (never the id of a paragraph it stands in), the default attributes and the Paragraph block, at the end of a line, at a soft line's start, over a selection and on an empty line, and the typing style is cleared (it used to mark the label). An atom below a soft line keeps its block and alignment through Delete, their Alt and Cmd variants, the empty edits, Shift+Return and Backspace at the end of the soft line (it took the soft line's block); Backspace at the start of a list or quote atom ends the list or quote for it, as for any list item. A fragment's atom pasted at the end of a head line whose soft lines follow, or at a soft line's start, goes in as `insert_atom` does it: on a line of its own with the defaults, no empty paragraph or soft line, and the soft lines behind it start a paragraph of their own that keeps the attributes they had. The soft lines (or text) that remain of a paragraph behind inserted text keep its attributes and block, whether or not the text ends in a break.
- **Turning ids on gives an id to every line that has none, in a field whose paragraphs carry attributes, and clears the undo history.** `set_paragraph_ids` does it when ids go from off to on (the history holds states from before there were ids; an app that keeps its own history with `set_history(false)` is not affected), and `set_paragraph_attrs` and `set_paragraph` do it while ids are on, so the order of the calls does not matter and `next_paragraph_id` ends the same.
- **A pasted atom agrees with `insert_atom` over selections too.** A selection within one line that ends at the line's end, with soft lines after it, leaves nothing of the line behind the pasted atom (`N | cd`, no empty line), and a selection that starts at a soft line's start replaces the soft break, as `insert_atom` does. Where a selection spans several lines, or replaces the whole text of a line with no soft line after it, a paste keeps the rule that the last line is the later paragraph (an empty line behind the atom, under its attributes) and `insert_atom` the rule that a join keeps the first.
